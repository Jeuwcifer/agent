Company & Core Business

Roxtec: A Swedish, global company providing sealing solutions for cable and pipe penetrations.

Protection: Solutions protect against fire, water, gas, and offer specialized functions like EMC, vibration damping, and explosion protection.

Roxtec Software Suite (Digital Ecosystem)

Overview: A "Digital Chain" managing the full product lifecycle: Design, Build, and Operation.

Components: Consists of three flagship products: Roxtec Transit Designer™, Roxtec Transit Build™, and Roxtec Transit Operate™.

1. Roxtec Transit Designer™ (Engineering)

Function: Free, web-based tool for designing cable/pipe transits.

Outputs: Generates manufacturer-approved drawings (2D DXF/PDF, 3D models), Bills of Material (BOM), and cable schedules.

Integration: Features an API and pushes data to Transit Build.

2. Roxtec Transit Build™ (Construction & Installation)

Purpose: SaaS product for managing construction, installation quality assurance (QA), and digital handovers.

Key Features:

Tracking: Real-time status reporting using a "traffic light system" (Yellow=Prep, Red=Issue, Green=Completed).

Mobile App: Android-based, supports offline capability, QR code scanning, and on-site photo documentation.

Geolocation: Tracks exact seal positions for asset management.

Workflow: Reduces work hours by 50-60% by digitizing the process from "Project Initiation" to "Digital Handover".

Pricing: 99 EUR/asset/month + 0.3 EUR per transit opening.

3. Roxtec Transit Operate™ (Maintenance)

Distinction: While Transit Build is for construction/handover, Transit Operate is used by asset owners for long-term maintenance.
